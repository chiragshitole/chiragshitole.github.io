Home & kitchen D2C brand

E-commerceMeta + Google1 Jan – 15 Sep 2026
The goal
Drive profitable website purchases for a traditional kitchenware brand with a loyal repeat base.
What I did
Put about 92% of Google spend into Performance Max for automated scale. On Meta, I ran dynamic catalog remarketing to keep the highest-value repeat buyers converting.
What happened
Orders dipped 6% year on year while gross sales rose 7%. Average order value went up, so growth came from efficiency and retention, not just more top-of-funnel traffic.
What I took from itPlatform ROAS and store ROAS tell different stories. Checking both against net sales gives a truer read on profit than either one alone.

Activewear D2C brand

E-commerceMeta + Google1 Jan – 15 Sep 2026
The goal
Scale new-customer purchases for an activewear brand through promotional, automated and catalog campaigns.
What I did
Made a Meta Advantage+ sales campaign the main engine: ₹5.66 L spend, 1,183 purchases, 4.46× ROAS. On top of that I ran offers (BOGO, flash sales, EMV) and added Google Performance Max for extra reach.
What happened
Gross sales more than doubled (+123%) and orders rose 67% year on year. Discounts rose 304%, so this was offer-led acquisition, deliberately part-funded by discounting.
What I took from itGrowth bought with discounts needs the same scrutiny as ad spend. Net sales and store-verified ROAS matter more than the headline gross number.

Real estate lead generation

Lead genMetaHistorical data
The goal
Generate enquiries at a sustainable cost across a portfolio of lead campaigns with different result types.
What I did
Ran 26 lead campaigns across form, call and Meta-lead objectives, judging each on volume, cost per result and its own lead definition.
What happened
5,737 lead results for ₹7.76 L, a weighted cost per lead of ₹135. Among the 12 biggest campaigns, CPL ranged from ₹48 to ₹788, so where the budget sat mattered more than the average.
What I took from itA lead isn't a purchase. I never compare lead results with purchase results, because the conversion definitions differ.
